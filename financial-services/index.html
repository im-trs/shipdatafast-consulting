Financial services

Financial data needs explainable answers.

A particular focus on financial services and fintech, with the same engineering principles applying to data-intensive teams beyond this sector.

Sector problems

  • Which identifier matches from one ledger or feed to another?
  • Which currency and effective date applies to a conversion or settlement record?
  • How are rounding, reversals, late postings and unresolved differences handled?

Engineering questions

These are engineering questions, not claims of regulatory approval, bank connectivity or compliance status. They guide how the data is mapped, checked and explained.
