Why Data Reconciliation Fails

Two datasets should match. They do not.

  • Missing rows.
  • Duplicate records.
  • Different column names.
  • Date formatting differences.
  • Type mismatches.
  • Changed values.

Finding the difference is easy. Proving why it happened, reproducing the comparison and making the result auditable is harder.
