Migration Sign-off Checklist.

What to agree before the mock load, check during reconciliation, and confirm before finance signs. A free 9-page PDF for programme directors, data migration leads, finance transformation leads, controllers and ERP consultants.

Download the checklist (PDF)

WHAT IS INSIDE

Three stages

  • Before mock load: named owner, source of truth, matching key, expected differences, tolerances, control totals, exception categories, rerun process.
  • During reconciliation: record counts, value totals, account-level and document-level comparison, missing records, duplicates, value breaks, timing differences.
  • Before sign-off: unresolved and explained differences, ownership, evidence, rerun status, finance acceptance.
