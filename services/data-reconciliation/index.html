Data reconciliation consulting

Find where your systems disagree.

Define how records should match, compare the relevant values and turn differences into an investigation list.

Scenarios

  • Migration verification between source and target systems.
  • Cross-system export comparison across distribution channels.
  • Source-to-target reconciliation for operational exceptions.

Matching contract

This work depends on the agreed matching contract: keys, field mapping, timing cut-off, normalisation, precision and tolerance rules.

Exceptions and checks

  • Missing records and unexpected duplicates.
  • Conflicting values and ambiguous keys.
  • Nulls, invalid inputs and date or currency differences.

Deliverables

Agreed comparison rules, repeatable checks, exception output and a clear handover with explanation of what did and did not match.
