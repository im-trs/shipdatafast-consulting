SCENARIOS

Where discrepancies arise

Database migrations, ledger synchronizations, payment provider reconciliations, and reporting feed comparisons all require proving that two separate data representations agree.

A successful reconciliation does not mean ignoring differences—it means cataloguing every mismatch with precision.

MATCHING CONTRACT

The matching specification

We work with your domain experts to agree on:

  • Key definitions: Primary vs composite keys and duplicate key handling.
  • Timing cut-offs: Resolving boundary and time-zone discrepancies.
  • Numeric tolerance: Rounding rules and currency conversion boundaries.
  • Exception classification: Differentiating missing records from conflicting values.
