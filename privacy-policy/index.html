Privacy policy

ShipDataFast operates a static website without a customer account system, checkout flow, lead database or analytics platform. This site is intended to provide information about the company, consulting services and product destinations.

When you contact ShipDataFast by email, the information you send is used to understand your enquiry and respond appropriately. We do not require account registration or credentials to view the public website.

Please do not send credentials, confidential records or production data unless you have agreed a secure process with the relevant contact. If you have a data-sharing need, confirm a safe channel before transmitting sensitive information.

We may process the following personal information when you contact us: your name, email address, organisation, and the content of your enquiry.

We use this information for business communication, scope clarification and project follow-up. We do not sell personal information and we do not use the site for marketing automation, product checkout or account management.

We may use website hosting and email infrastructure operated by third parties to deliver the website and respond to enquiries. Those services are used strictly for website operation and contact handling.

This website may use standard server logs to monitor technical availability and reliability. We do not add a buyer-tracking system to the site as part of this static release.

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed to respond to the enquiry, fulfill the agreed business purpose, or satisfy legal or accounting obligations.

If you want to ask about your data or request that a contact message be updated or deleted, please contact us at info@shipdatafast.com.
