Privacy Policy

We operate a static website with zero tracking scripts, cookies, or third-party analytical tags.

1. Overview

ShipDataFast (operated by TR Seeds Ltd) is committed to protecting your privacy. This static website does not execute tracking cookies, user profiling, or third-party analytics scripts.

2. Information Collected via Email

When you contact us via email (info@shipdatafast.com), we collect only the personal information and message details you choose to provide in order to respond to your technical enquiry.

3. Data Security

We advise that you do not send confidential business records, database passwords, or unredacted personal identifiers in initial unencrypted email communications.

4. Contact

For questions regarding privacy, please contact: info@shipdatafast.com.
