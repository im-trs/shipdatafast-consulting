ABOUT SHIPDATAFAST

Specialist financial data reconciliation.

ShipDataFast grew from a clear conviction: when financial systems move critical records, organizations cannot settle for "probably correct." You need deterministic proof that every position, balance, and transaction arrived intact.

01 / THE CONSULTANCY

Specialist engineering practice

ShipDataFast is a specialist consultancy helping financial-services institutions, fintechs, and technical data teams verify that critical data moves accurately across migrations, integrations, and operational pipelines.

We combine senior hands-on data engineering with automated matching workflows and proprietary local-first tooling. We are not a generic digital agency, and we are not just a CSV viewer: we provide mathematical, reproducible assurance for high-stakes enterprise systems.

Business identity: ShipDataFast is a trading name of TR Seeds Ltd.

02 / FOUNDER & PEDIGREE

Hands-on senior engineering

ShipDataFast was founded by senior data engineer Ivano Mannella, drawing upon extensive hands-on experience across software engineering, financial transactional systems, test automation, data validation, and complex migration assurance.

Having navigated the intricacies of core trading platforms, investment systems, market-data feeds, and transactional ledgers, Ivano leads directly on architecture, matching contracts, and deterministic test suites.

Where specific platforms (such as Murex, Aladdin, or Bloomberg ecosystems) are referenced across our practice, they represent technologies and environments personally encountered in prior professional delivery.

03 / DELIVERY MODEL

How we deliver

We work in bounded, high-impact consulting engagements with concrete deliverables: mapped schemas, reproducible automated checks, transparent matching contracts, and clean handovers to your internal engineering team.

Our engagements are structured to preserve your autonomy. Tooling runs within your secure perimeter, and all matching specifications remain transparent and auditable.
