About

The engineering behind ShipDataFast.

ShipDataFast. Data engineering consulting and software from TR Seeds Ltd.

Company first

ShipDataFast is a data engineering consulting company focused on making data flows easier to understand, validate and reconcile. The company works with teams dealing with source-to-target movement, data quality questions and differences between systems.

Founder

Ivano Mannella is the founder and hands-on engineer behind ShipDataFast. The company’s public positioning reflects current consulting and product work without overstating any historical claim.

Relevant experience

Specific claims should be grounded in concrete project work and recorded role context. Where dates or historical product work are discussed, they should remain attributable and separated from template or marketing history.

Delivery model

The delivery model is direct, practical and evidence-driven: define the data problem, map the movement, check the edge cases and hand over an understandable result with clear ownership.
