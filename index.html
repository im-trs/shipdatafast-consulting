THE RECONCILIATION CHALLENGE

Your systems moved the data. Did they preserve its meaning?

System A says 10,004,219 records. System B says 10,004,101. We find the other 118.

Pipelines execute without throwing code exceptions, yet balances diverge, rounding rules create subtle drift, and critical position rows vanish during migration. Typical situations we check for:

  • 12 million records migrated: Can you independently prove all 12 million arrived without distortion?
  • Aggregate totals match, but positions do not: Compensating errors mask offsetting discrepancies.
  • Transformation shifts: Records exist in both systems, but values silently altered during ETL.
  • Vendor feed schema mutations: Upstream feed changes break downstream consumption silently.
  • Timing cut-off differences: Transaction cut-offs and timezone differences generate false discrepancies.
  • Currency & decimal precision: Discrepancies caused by floating-point rounding vs fixed decimal ledgers.
  • Composite keys & disparate models: Legacy and target systems represent the same business object differently.

We provide deterministic reconciliation and migration assurance so your stakeholders have auditable evidence.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Direct technical answers regarding our consulting scope, validation rules, and reconciliation methodology.

What is the difference between data validation and data reconciliation?

Data reconciliation compares corresponding records across two separate datasets (such as source and target systems) to detect discrepancies and missing items. In contrast, data validation evaluates a single dataset against explicit criteria (such as required fields, unique keys, and numeric thresholds) without requiring a second comparison dataset.

When should a company reconcile data instead of validating it?

You should use data reconciliation during system migrations, cross-platform integrations, or ledger synchronizations where data exists in two places and must agree. Use data validation at data ingestion boundaries to enforce schema constraints, field formatting, and business rules before data reaches downstream pipelines.

Does ShipDataFast consulting or software require uploading data to external cloud servers?

No. ShipDataFast consulting and specialized tools are designed for zero-trust, local, or client-controlled environments. Workflows and comparisons run directly within your designated infrastructure, ensuring confidential financial records, client identifiers, and confidential data never leave your secure perimeter.

What data formats and sources are supported for consulting and reconciliation?

Our engineering workflows support relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server), delimited formats (CSV, TSV), JSON/REST APIs, and warehouse exports. Matching contracts and validation suites are configured to fit your specific source-to-target architecture.

How does ShipDataFast differ from large monolithic close-management platforms?

Unlike broad accounting platforms focused on month-end financial close, ShipDataFast specializes in engineering pipeline integrity and cross-system migration checks. We deliver targeted, verifiable data engineering workflows in weeks rather than requiring multi-month provisioning cycles or long-term platform subscriptions.

Why choose specialized data engineering consulting over general IT consultancy firms?

Generalist consulting firms typically deploy large, multi-tier teams focused on broad advisory work. ShipDataFast provides direct, hands-on senior engineering with dedicated matching and validation tooling, solving concrete data discrepancy problems with deterministic code and rapid handovers.

How do you prevent vendor lock-in for data validation and reconciliation workflows?

Every engagement prioritizes client autonomy. We provide fully documented matching specifications, transparent rule configurations, and auditable deliverables that run inside your perimeter, ensuring your internal team retains complete operational control without lock-in to our tooling.
