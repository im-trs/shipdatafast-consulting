Your systems moved the data. Did they preserve its meaning?

Data flows fail in subtle ways. Pipelines execute without errors, but values shift, timing cut-offs misalign, and critical records drop silently between source and target.

We work with teams facing three practical challenges: migration results that don't match source systems, transformed figures that require rigorous validation checks, and manual spreadsheet reconciliations that cannot scale reliably.
